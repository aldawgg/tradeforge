# TradeForge

## Product purpose

A futures trading journal for serious independent traders. Helps users log trades, track P&L, analyze performance by setup, manage prop firm evaluations, and review mistakes. The journal is the source of truth for a trader's edge.

## Register

product

## Users

Retail futures traders, primarily MES and MNQ. Many are prop firm traders working through funded evaluations. They care about consistency, rule compliance, and improving their setups. They are data-aware but not developers. They use the app daily for logging and weekly for review.

## Brand tone

Clean, direct, serious. No hype. No gamification. No crypto aesthetics. Speaks like a head trader, not a marketing team.

## Anti-references

- Crypto/NFT neon dashboards
- Casino-style color overload
- TradingView complexity
- Financial app navy-and-gold clichés
- "Gain" / "rocket" / "moon" copy

## Strategic principles

- Journal first, analytics second. Getting trades logged is the primary job.
- Accuracy over encouragement. If a trader lost money, show it clearly.
- Prop firm traders need rule visibility (daily loss limit, consistency score, eval status) without hardcoding any specific firm's rules.
- Setup analysis belongs in Analytics, not the dashboard.
- Keep it MVP-first. No copy trading, broker APIs, AI, or backtesting yet.
